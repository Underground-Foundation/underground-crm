{% comment %} The visitor's name, laid out as a Bootstrap form grid instead of one field per row. Prefix, first, preferred, middle, last and suffix are all short and together read as a single thing — a name — so stacking them vertically both wastes the page and hides how they relate to one another. The cells come from the form (RegistrationForm.naming_fields), not from a fixed list of field names, for two reasons. A registration form's fields are keyed by the UUID of the page block that produced them, so they cannot be addressed here by the Person field they write to; and an editor may delete any of the naming blocks, so the grid has to render whichever of them the page actually carries. Each cell's width comes from PERSON_NAME_FIELD_COLUMNS in blocks.py, and the row simply wraps when the widths overflow it — so a grid missing some of its fields still lines up, and the widths of the cells that remain do not have to add up to a whole number of rows. {% endcomment %} {% if form.naming_fields %}
